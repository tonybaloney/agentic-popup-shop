Returns & Refund Policy

Effective for all orders placed at Zava Shop

Return Window

Items may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. Returns requested after 30 days cannot be accepted.

Eligible Return Reasons

  • Defective or damaged item – the item arrived broken, torn, or non-functional.
  • Wrong item received – you received a different product than what you ordered.
  • Item doesn't match description – the product is materially different from its listing (colour, material, features).
  • Size or fit issue – clothing or footwear does not fit as expected based on the size guide.
  • Changed mind (within 30 days) – you no longer want the item, provided it is unused and in original packaging.
  • Duplicate order – you accidentally placed the same order twice.

Ineligible Return Reasons

  • Items that have been worn, washed, or used beyond initial inspection.
  • Items without original tags or packaging.
  • Perishable or consumable goods (e.g., food, beverages).
  • Gift cards or store-credit vouchers.
  • Items marked as Final Sale or Non-Returnable on the product page.
  • Items returned after the 30-day return window.

Full-Order Returns

If you wish to return all items in your order, you must upload a photo showing the items placed inside or on top of a cardboard box, ready for shipment. Our AI verification system will review the photo before approving the return.

Partial Returns

You may return individual items from an order without a photo. Select the items you would like to return and provide a reason.

Refund Processing

Once a return is approved, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. You will receive an email confirmation when the refund is processed.

Tip: Providing a clear, specific reason that matches one of the eligible categories above helps us process your return faster.
